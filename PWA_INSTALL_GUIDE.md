# A C A D I A Motion Tracker Installation Guide

The scanner is a Progressive Web App. Install it from its HTTPS address rather than an app store. After the first online load, its interface and mission files are cached for offline use.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the scanner address in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Scroll down and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Keep the name **A.C.A.D.I.A.** and tap **Add**.
5. Launch the scanner from the new Home Screen icon.
6. Complete one tutorial while the phone is online.

Before the show, set media volume, choose a suitable brightness, disable Low Power Mode, and set Auto Lock to Never. Use Guided Access to prevent visitors from leaving the app. Older iPhones may not provide browser vibration, so the scanner always includes audible cues.

## Install on Samsung or Android

1. Open the scanner address in Chrome.
2. Open the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Launch the scanner from the new Home Screen icon.
4. Complete one tutorial while the phone is online.

Before the show, set media volume and brightness, prevent screen locking, and use app pinning to keep visitors in the scanner.

## Prepare every show phone

- Charge the battery fully.
- Open the app online once after every update.
- Close and reopen the installed app before testing.
- Run the tutorial and confirm sound.
- Put the phone in a rugged case with a wrist strap.
- Keep one spare phone ready and charged.
